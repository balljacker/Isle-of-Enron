#Event

The **Thaw Festival** marks the end of Enron's harsh winter. Villagers from every region gather in [[Nevrah]] to celebrate the melting snows and the promise of easier days ahead.

### Activities
- Communal feasts featuring preserved meats and the season's first fresh greens.
- Bonfires lit by [[Nevran Flame Keepers]] to symbolize warmth returning to the land.
- Friendly competitions of ice carving, storytelling, and magical duels.

### Significance
Beyond simple revelry, the festival renews trade ties between settlements and honors the spirits believed to guard the mountains.
