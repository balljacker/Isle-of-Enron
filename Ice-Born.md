#Race #Magic

The **Ice-Born** are beings infused with elemental frost from birth. Their skin carries patterns of pale blue or white, and they radiate a faint chill wherever they go. Most originate from the frozen regions of [[Enron]], but wandering Ice-Born are not unheard of.

### Traits
- Resistant to cold climates and ice-based spells.
- Capable of channeling frost into weapons or tools, forming temporary constructs of solid ice.
- Often aloof, valuing tradition and personal resilience.

### Culture
Ice-Born communities prize endurance and respect the forces of nature that forged them. Clans gather in stone halls carved into icy cliffs, celebrating deeds of survival and feats of magical prowess.
