### Factions :
#### The Frostbound Accord
An alliance of settlements across the mountains, dedicated to mutual defense
and trade in enchanted ice. Members include [[Nevrah]] and several remote
mining camps.

#### The Crimson Merchants
Traveling caravans that cross the [[Southern Redsands]], dealing in exotic
spices and metals. They maintain the outpost of [[Vorsec]] as a key waypoint.
