#Biome

#### Overview
The **Northern Plains** stretch across Enron's tundra-like north. Windswept grasslands and scattered evergreen copses make travel arduous but beautiful.

#### Features
- Rolling hills often blanketed in snow for much of the year.
- Nomadic tribes guide herds of hardy beasts across seasonal grazing routes.
- Frequent auroras illuminate the night, said to be the souls of ancient warriors.
