#Biome

#### Overview
The **Eastern Cascades** form a rugged line of snow-capped peaks. Valleys cradle quiet villages like [[Nevrah]], while treacherous passes hide mineral riches.

#### Features
- Steep cliffs and narrow trails, often blocked by seasonal avalanches.
- Hot springs bubble near geothermal vents, offering respite from the cold.
- Alpine forests shelter rare medicinal herbs prized across Enron.
