#Location #Settlement #Village

#### Overview
Tremirn rests on the open steppe of the Northern Plains. It began as a seasonal camp for nomads but has grown into a permanent trading post where caravans regroup before crossing the tundra.

#### People and Trade
Residents are hardy and pragmatic, trading animal hides, dried meats, and guiding services. Visitors often come seeking the tales of aurora-watchers who claim to hear whispers in the night sky.
