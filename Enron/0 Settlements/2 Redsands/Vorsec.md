##### [[Outpost]] within the [[2 Southern Redsands]] of [[Enron]]

#### Overview
Vorsec sits at the edge of a narrow canyon rich in mineral deposits. Originally a temporary camp, it now serves as a bustling hub for miners and merchants.

#### Daily Life
The settlement relies on underground cisterns to store precious water. Market stalls crowd the central square, trading ore, stone carvings, and desert spices brought in by caravan.
